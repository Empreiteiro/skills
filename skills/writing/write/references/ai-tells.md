# AI tells — what never survives in the final text

A quality check, run **last**, before handing the text over. A tell from this list in the final text is a
reason to rewrite, not to argue.

Two halves, because **Portuguese AI tells are not translations of English ones.** A model writing in
Portuguese has tics of its own, inherited from Brazilian corporate blog prose, and a list made for
English catches none of them. Use the half that matches the text; section 3 applies to both.

The English half is an operational summary of the two generic skills, which remain the long reference
with before-and-after examples: `../../humanizer/SKILL.md` and `../../no-ai-slop/SKILL.md`.

Where an entry mentions the voice DNA, the brand may allow that pattern within a budget
(`voice-dna.md` §6). Without that permission, the tell is cut.

# 1. English

## Banned words

delve · foster · leverage · utilize · facilitate · empower · streamline · robust · cutting-edge ·
paradigm shift · game changer · tapestry · realm · beacon · multifaceted · meticulous · intricate ·
paramount · transformative · elevate · embark · supercharge · harness · ever-evolving · vibrant ·
nestled · in the heart of · boasts · showcase · underscore · testament · pivotal · landscape (abstract) ·
interplay · seamless

## Empty phrases

it's worth noting · it's important to note · at the end of the day · when it comes to · at its core ·
in today's world · in the age of · the reality is · the truth is · in terms of · going forward ·
in this article · let's dive in · here's the thing · let me be clear · I'll be honest

## Patterns

| Pattern | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Inflated importance**: "stands as a testament", "marks a pivotal moment" | state the fact and let the reader judge |
| **Shallow -ing analysis**: "highlighting the team's commitment" | cut it, or turn it into a real consequence |
| **Not X but Y** | say Y (the voice DNA may budget one) |
| **Colon reveal**: "The best part: it learns." | make it a plain sentence (the voice DNA may budget one) |
| **Rule of three** | cut to two or go to four |
| **Fake-strong verbs**: "serves as", "boasts" | `is`, `has` |
| **Synonym cycling**: protagonist / main character / hero | repeat the right word |
| **Weasel attribution**: "experts agree", "studies show" | name the source or cut |
| **Faux insight**: "what most people get wrong" | cut the setup and make the claim on its own |
| **Dramatic fragmentation**: "That's it. That's the whole thing." | one complete sentence |
| **Summary-recap ending**: "In conclusion", "Ultimately" | end on the last fact |
| **Fake-profound kicker** | delete it; don't improve the metaphor |
| **Em dash cluster** | cut the cluster and the decorative dash (the voice DNA may keep an isolated one) |

# 2. Portuguese

Each tell is quoted as it appears in Portuguese text, followed by an English gloss.

## Corporate-blog openings

| Tell | Why it gives the text away |
|---|---|
| "Em um mundo cada vez mais…" ("In an increasingly … world") | sets the scene for the universe instead of naming the thing |
| "Nos dias de hoje" ("Nowadays") · "Atualmente, com o avanço da tecnologia" ("Today, with the advance of technology") | the same, and says nothing anyone can check |
| "Você já parou pra pensar…?" ("Have you ever stopped to think…?") · "E se eu te dissesse que…" ("What if I told you…") | a rhetorical question the text then answers itself |
| "Vamos falar sobre" ("Let's talk about") · "Neste artigo, vamos explorar" ("In this article, we'll explore") | announces the text instead of writing it |
| "A verdade é que" ("The truth is") · "O fato é que" ("The fact is") | sets up a reveal that never comes |

**Fix:** the first sentence is the claim. Cut everything before it.

## Connectors a model overuses

"além disso" (furthermore) · "ademais" (moreover) · "outrossim" (likewise, archaic) · "vale ressaltar" /
"vale destacar" (it's worth highlighting) · "é importante notar" (it's important to note) ·
"nesse sentido" (in this sense) · "dessa forma" (this way) · "por conseguinte" (consequently) ·
"em suma" (in short) · "por fim, mas não menos importante" (last but not least)

One per page passes. Two consecutive paragraphs that open with a connector is a model's signature.

## Inflated words

"robusto" (robust) · "poderoso" (powerful) · "inovador" (innovative) · "revolucionário" (revolutionary) ·
"transformador" (transformative) · "disruptivo" (disruptive) · "otimizado" (optimized) · "eficiente"
(efficient, as vague praise) · "estratégico" (strategic) · "fundamental" · "crucial" · "essencial"
(essential) · "significativo" (significant) · "impactante" (impactful) · "potencializar" (to boost) ·
"alavancar" (to leverage) · "elevar a outro patamar" (to take to the next level) · "desbloquear"
(to unlock, figurative) · "empoderar" (to empower) · "maximizar" (to maximize)

All of them describe importance instead of saying what the thing does.

## The "it's not just about X" contrast

"Não se trata apenas de X, é sobre Y" ("It's not just about X, it's about Y"), in every variant:
"não é só X, é Y" ("it's not only X, it's Y"), "mais do que X, é Y" ("more than X, it's Y"),
"não é sobre X. É sobre Y." ("It's not about X. It's about Y.")

The voice DNA may budget one plain contrast. This entry is about the excess and about the inflated
variant ("it's not just about writing, it's about transforming…"), which no brand voice allows.

## Groups of three

"Rápido, simples e eficiente." ("Fast, simple and efficient.") · "Clareza, propósito e impacto."
("Clarity, purpose and impact.")

A model closes in threes because three sounds complete. Close in two or in four.

## Explanatory gerund at the end of a sentence

"…, garantindo mais produtividade" ("…, ensuring more productivity") · "…, refletindo o compromisso da
empresa" ("…, reflecting the company's commitment") · "…, proporcionando uma experiência única"
("…, providing a unique experience") · "…, permitindo que você foque no que importa" ("…, letting you
focus on what matters")

It fakes depth on top of a simple fact. Cut the gerund, or turn it into a clause with a subject.

## School-essay endings

"Em resumo" ("In summary") · "Em suma" ("In short") · "Concluindo" ("To conclude") · "Portanto, fica
claro que" ("Therefore, it is clear that") · a last paragraph that repeats the whole text · a closing
metaphor such as "afinal, escrever é um ato humano" ("after all, writing is a human act").

End on the last concrete fact or on the action.

## Passive voice and missing subject

"O texto é processado pela plataforma." ("The text is processed by the platform.") · "Foram
implementadas melhorias." ("Improvements were implemented.") · "É possível observar que…" ("It is
possible to observe that…")

Whoever acts has a name: you, the product, the team.

## Literal translation from English

"em ordem de" (a calque of "in order to"; Portuguese says "para") · "isso faz sentido" used as agreement
("that makes sense") · "no final do dia" ("at the end of the day") · "tem tudo a ver com" ("it's all
about") · "nós temos você coberto" ("we've got you covered") · "não hesite em" ("don't hesitate to") ·
"sinta-se à vontade para" ("feel free to") · "esperamos que isso ajude" ("we hope this helps")

Inherited from English training data, and the tic that most often gives away AI text in Portuguese.

## Chatbot courtesy

"Espero que isso ajude!" ("Hope this helps!") · "Ótima pergunta!" ("Great question!") · "Claro!"
("Sure!") · "Fico à disposição" ("I remain at your disposal") · "Vamos juntos nessa jornada" ("Let's go
on this journey together") · "Ops! Algo deu errado 😕" ("Oops! Something went wrong") · any exclamation
mark · section emoji (🚀 ✨ 💡)

## Vague source

"especialistas afirmam" ("experts say") · "estudos mostram" ("studies show") · "o mercado aponta" ("the
market indicates") · "muitos acreditam" ("many believe") · "dados recentes indicam" ("recent data
indicates")

Name the source or cut. Never invent a source to save the sentence.

# 3. Both languages

## Chat formatting leaking into the product

- Bold mid-sentence for emphasis.
- Emoji in a section heading.
- A bulleted list where two sentences would do.
- A heading over a two-line section.
- Em dashes in a cluster (two in a short paragraph). An isolated dash may be the brand's; check
  `voice-dna.md` §4.

## Portability test

If a sentence could move unchanged to another company, product or country, it is filler. Replace it
with a fact, a mechanism, a real number or a judgment that only fits this brand.

## How to check, in practice

1. Scan for the word lists above. It is the fastest step and catches most tells.
2. Count groups of three, binary contrasts, colon reveals and dashes. Compare them with the budgets in
   `voice-dna.md` §6.
3. Read the first and the last sentence on their own. Does the first make a claim? Is the last concrete?
4. Run the portability test on every generic sentence.
5. A tell left? Rewrite. Do not negotiate with the list.

**When unsure** whether something is an AI tell or the writer's real voice, flag it as a likely variation
and ask. Never silently "fix" a line that may be the brand's signature.
